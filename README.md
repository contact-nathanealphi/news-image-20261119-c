A clean infographic front page that sums up the world's news of
Thursday 19 November 2026: a self-drawn world map with eight numbered
story points, eight numbered story cards, and a numbers strip.

- `tools/render.py` draws `assets/entry.png` (1024x1024) with Pillow
  from hand-written coordinates — no photos, no network, no randomness.
- `main.py` (standard library only) copies `assets/entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`).

Stories, all drawn from reports published 19 November 2026: the
three-way Shenzhen summit, strikes across the Gulf, the Kerch Bridge,
Anthropic's Nasdaq debut, floods in Gaza, Israel's government talks,
the ICJ case on Myanmar, and Voyager 1.
